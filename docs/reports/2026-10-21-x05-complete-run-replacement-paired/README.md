# X05 complete-run replacement paired evaluation

This five-pair Release public matrix compares baseline `ad8199a6d71d96d9b2e7d4e08df93d8e15d90b75` with candidate `29097cb42e6f91818fdc287b47c37620478bc8ba` using the unchanged v1 trace (`docs/reports/2026-10-21-x05-middle-insert-v2-paired/raw/aggregate.json` records the v2 trace hash; this matrix uses the runner's default v1 trace, whose hash is recorded in [`raw/aggregate.json`](raw/aggregate.json)). All 54 public workload state and operation fingerprints matched. This was a public-only matrix: it did not rerun native benchmarks or recovery cases. The full environment, per-call records, aggregate, and commands are in [`raw/`](raw/).

In the public 10-KB replacement workload after 3,000 prior replacements, the median paired mean-latency ratio was 0.771, about 23% lower than baseline. The median across the five per-run p50 values was 13.04 ms baseline and 13.01 ms candidate; the median of paired p50 ratios was 0.907. Median per-run p99 values were 21.61 ms and 20.42 ms, while the median paired p99 ratio was 1.020. Individual pairs varied widely, with candidate p99 ratios from 0.809 to 1.687. Thus the mean improved modestly, while the typical call changed little and the tail did not improve consistently. The per-run p99 remains over ten times X05's 2 ms ceiling, and mean latency remains far from the 5x target.

The 10-KB no-history public replacement mean ratio was 1.055, a 5.5% median increase within the 10% mean guard. Its median p50 and p99 ratios were 1.114 and 1.123. The byte-control aged replacement mean ratio was 0.831, with p99 ratio 1.203. Those results are mixed and noisy; one-minute host load ranged roughly 3.9–11.7 on the powersave host. Treat this matrix as directional evidence only. It does not test the 5-KB/50%-deletion target and does not establish X05 acceptance.

This candidate adds an incremental path only for replacement of one complete visible retained run at a proven fragment boundary. Partial-run, multi-run, and ambiguous edits retain the reconstruction fallback. The small mean improvement on the public aged replacement case, with almost no p50 or p99 change, indicates that this narrow path has not removed the dominant cost in the target workload. Continue with counted fallback/work evidence and broader transactional splicing.
