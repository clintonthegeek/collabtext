# X05 ancestry-walk optimization evaluation

This five-pair full Release matrix compares baseline `0fbd15bbad5a8488e78cce56fa0ddb133f225b35` with the source tree at HEAD `c2a82758a45224ceec17fe42313ed04eeb053bab` plus its five-file working-tree diff. The exact candidate diff SHA-256 is `55516bbf618e73525d284f57d1846da0e79406f0857eb4d82d8a6817b7a0dc85`; `environment.json` records source and harness hashes. The matrix includes public calls, native benchmark suites, and 512/2048-operation recovery cases. All 56 public state and operation fingerprints matched, and all five native suites passed 14/14 QtTest cases. Raw runs and build logs are in [`raw/`](raw/).

The public aged 10-KB replacement after 3,000 prior edits improved from a median per-run mean of 10.447 ms to 0.165 ms across pairs (median paired ratio 0.0157, about 64x); median per-run p99 fell from 12.259 ms to 0.366 ms (ratio 0.0292). The matched byte-control case showed a similar gain. The 3,000-run random middle-insert case improved from 3.508 ms to 0.041 ms mean per call (ratio 0.0124, about 81x). The four no-history public replacement workloads also improved; their median mean ratios ranged from 0.389 to 0.505. These results exceed X05's aged-edit mean and p99 goals for this candidate.

The native mixed-deletion goal remains unmet. For the 5-KB/50%-tombstone workload, the median of the five paired per-edit mean ratios was 0.844, about 1.18x faster, below the required 2x. The related 3-KB and 8-KB cases measured about 1.11x and 1.46x. The 90%-tombstone 5-KB workload improved about 1.15x. Host governor was powersave, so small differences are directional; the large aged public gain was consistent across the five pairs.

The change instruments identity ancestry traversal, rules out impossible same-actor ancestry by counter order, and removes the reverse ancestry walk after the forward ancestry proof. A focused aged diagnostic trace now reports ancestry steps with median 0 and p95 2 over all 200 calls; the exact-run 73-call subset has median 2 and p95 3, versus thousands of steps before the change. Debug and Release model-sensitive routes passed 12/12; serial leak-enabled ASan/UBSan passed 11/11 outside the sandbox; the diagnostics-enabled target passed 20/20. These are local correctness and work-count checks, not consumer acceptance.

X05 remains in progress. The aged public workload targets now pass for this candidate, but the mixed 5-KB/50%-tombstone 2x target remains open. Full X05 routed Debug/Release and relocated installed-package gates are still required. This report records an interim candidate with uncommitted source at measurement time; it is not a new immutable adoption tag.
